## Problem Statement

What is in the way, and who it is in the way of. A reader who arrives here years
later has none of today's context, so say what was true at the time rather than
what everyone knew.

## Context

The forces in play. Technical ones, such as what the codebase already does and
what it would cost to do otherwise; and social ones, such as what contributors
expect and what other projects in the same position have settled on.

### Exemplary Prior Art

Who else has decided this, and what they chose. Link to it.

### Alternatives Considered

The options that were real, and what each would have cost. An option nobody
seriously weighed does not belong here; an option that lost on one point belongs
here with that point named.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and as briefly as it can be said. This is
the part people will quote back, so it says one thing.

## Results

What changed once the decision landed, including anything that turned out worse
than expected. This section is written after the fact, not with the rest.

## Next Steps

What is left to do, as a checklist. An empty list is a decision that landed
whole, which is worth saying.
